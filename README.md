# Lenskart Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Sales Analysis Dashboard built using Power BI and Zoho Analytics. The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights, enabling stakeholders to monitor sales performance, financial metrics, and product category trends through interactive visualizations.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Evaluate financial KPIs and revenue trends.
- Identify top-performing states, categories, and sales channels.
- Understand customer demographics and payment preferences.
- Support data-driven business decisions through interactive dashboards.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Power BI
- Zoho Analytics
- Power Query
- DAX
- Microsoft Excel

# 🔍 Key Insights

## Overview
- Generated ₹784.36M in total revenue.
- Online sales channel contributed the highest revenue.
- Kerala, Madhya Pradesh, and Odisha were the top-performing states.
- Sales volume peaked in 2023 and 2024.

## Finance
- Male customers contributed 33.5% of total revenue.
- Eyeglasses and Contact Lenses generated the highest revenue.
- Net Banking and Debit Card were the most preferred payment methods.

## Category
- Eyeglasses held the highest market share (25.16%).
- Online stores offered the highest discounts.
- Eyeglasses recorded peak sales in March, while Sunglasses peaked in July.
- February recorded the lowest sales.
